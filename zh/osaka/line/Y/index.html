从西梅田到住之江公園，共11站
  1. Y11西梅田Nishi-umedaSophisticated business district anchored by the Herbis and Breeze towers.
  2. Y12肥後橋HigobashiOffice district facing Nakanoshima across the Tosabori River.
  3. Y13本町HommachiHeart of the Semba district, lined with wholesale merchants and offices.
  4. Y14四ツ橋YotsubashiLively downtown corner within walking distance of Shinsaibashi and Amerika-mura.
  5. Y15なんばNambaDotonbori and the iconic Glico sign anchor the heart of Minami.
  6. Y16大国町DaikokuchoNear Imamiya Ebisu Shrine and kushikatsu eateries, close to Shinsekai.
  7. Y17花園町HanazonochoOld-fashioned neighborhood along the long Tsurumibashi shopping arcade.
  8. Y18岸里KishinosatoDirectly connected to the Nishinari ward office, with a Nankai Line station within walking distance.
  9. Y19玉出TamadeBusy intersection on Route 26 sharing the same working-class feel as neighboring Nishinari.
  10. Y20北加賀屋Kita-kagayaFormer shipyard reborn as a creative arts hub at Creative Center Osaka.
  11. Y21住之江公園Suminoe-koenTransfer point to the Nanko Port Town Line (Neutram), right next to the Suminoe boat race stadium.
