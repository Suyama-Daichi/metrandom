从天神橋筋六丁目到天下茶屋，共10站
  1. K11天神橋筋六丁目Tenjimbashisuji-rokuchomeNorthern end of Tenjinbashisuji, Japan's longest shopping arcade.
  2. K12扇町OgimachiHome to Ogimachi Park and the Kids Plaza Osaka museum.
  3. K13南森町Minami-morimachiOsaka Temmangu shrine and the mile-long Tenjinbashi-suji shopping arcade.
  4. K14北浜KitahamaFinance district anchored by the Osaka Exchange and historic Tekijuku school.
  5. K15堺筋本町Sakaisuji-hommachiDirectly linked to Semba Center Building, hub of the textile wholesale district.
  6. K16長堀橋NagahoribashiSteps from Amerika-mura and the Shinsaibashi-suji shopping arcade.
  7. K17日本橋NippombashiGateway to Den Den Town, Osaka's electronics and otaku culture district.
  8. K18恵美須町EbisuchoRetro old-town feel by Tsutenkaku Tower and Shinsekai.
  9. K19動物園前Dobutsuen-maeRight by Tsutenkaku Tower, Shinsekai and the retro Janjan Yokocho arcade.
  10. K20天下茶屋TengachayaOld-town stop tied to Hideyoshi's teahouse legend, linking with Nankai lines.
