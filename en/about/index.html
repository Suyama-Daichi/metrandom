About this site

Metro Station Gacha (https://metrandom.com) is a station roulette that picks one random subway station at the press of a button. It is made and run by an individual to help people choose a place to get off, take a weekend walk or explore a new neighborhood.

It currently covers three cities:

  • Tokyo: all 9 Tokyo Metro lines and all 4 Toei Subway lines (291 stations)
  • Osaka: all 9 Osaka Metro lines (134 stations)
  • Nagoya: all 6 lines of the Transportation Bureau City of Nagoya (100 stations)

Station counts are per line (a transfer station counts once for each line).

Operator

Donchan (individual)

About the information on this site

Station names, station numbers and line colors are based on information published by each railway operator at the time of creation. The one-line station guides are written by the operator. Please check each operator's official website for the latest information such as renamed stations and service status.

This is an unofficial fan site and is not affiliated with Tokyo Metro Co., Ltd., the Tokyo Metropolitan Bureau of Transportation, Osaka Metro Co., Ltd. or the Transportation Bureau City of Nagoya.

Advertising and affiliate links

To cover running costs, the site shows Google AdSense ads and Rakuten affiliate links; affiliate links are labeled "PR". See the Privacy Policy for details.

Contact

For questions or to report incorrect information, please open an issue on the GitHub repository. The operator (Donchan) will review and respond.
