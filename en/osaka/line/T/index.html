Tanimachi Line Stations

26 stations from Dainichi to Yao-minami
  1. T11Dainichi大日A terminus where a huge AEON Mall meets the Osaka Monorail.
  2. T12Moriguchi守口Close to Panasonic's headquarters, with the city hall and hospital nearby.
  3. T13Taishibashi-imaichi太子橋今市A residential area near the Yodo River park and Osaka Institute of Technology.
  4. T14Senbayashi-omiya千林大宮Home to Senbayashi Shopping Street, once dubbed Japan's cheapest arcade.
  5. T15Sekime-takadono関目高殿A residential area of homes and condos, with historic sites like Sekime Shrine.
  6. T16Noe-uchindai野江内代A quiet residential area with a shopping street stretching toward the Yodo River.
  7. T17Miyakojima都島Heart of Miyakojima Ward, home to Osaka City General Medical Center.
  8. T18Tenjimbashisuji-rokuchome天神橋筋六丁目Northern end of Tenjinbashisuji, Japan's longest shopping arcade.
  9. T19Nakazakicho中崎町Retro Showa-era backstreets lined with cafes in renovated old houses.
  10. T20Higashi-umeda東梅田Gateway to Umeda's underground malls, right by the Ohatsu Tenjin shopping street.
  11. T21Minami-morimachi南森町Osaka Temmangu shrine and the mile-long Tenjinbashi-suji shopping arcade.
  12. T22Temmabashi天満橋Hachikenya-hama riverbank on the Okawa, a historic boat-transport hub of Water City Osaka.
  13. T23Tanimachi-yonchome谷町四丁目Gateway to Osaka Castle Park and the Osaka Museum of History.
  14. T24Tanimachi-rokuchome谷町六丁目Hilly Uemachi Plateau streets and the retro Karahori shopping arcade.
  15. T25Tanimachi-kyuchome谷町九丁目Ikutama and Kozu shrines amid the historic temple lanes of Shitadera-machi.
  16. T26Shitennoji-mae-yuhigaoka四天王寺前夕陽ヶ丘Temple-lined hilltop famed for sunsets seen through a stone torii gate.
  17. T27Tennoji天王寺Abeno Harukas tower, Shitennoji Temple and Tennoji Park all nearby.
  18. T28Abeno阿倍野Bustling shopping district anchored by Japan's tallest skyscraper.
  19. T29Fuminosato文の里A calm residential neighborhood dotted with schools and small parks.
  20. T30Tanabe田辺Old-fashioned neighborhood home to Momogaike Park, known for peach and cherry blossoms.
  21. T31Komagawa-nakano駒川中野Komagawa shopping street, nicknamed Flower Road for its greenery.
  22. T32Hirano平野Ancient moated settlement dating back to the Heian era, one of Osaka's oldest towns.
  23. T33Kire-uriwari喜連瓜破Family-friendly area near the Yamato River, with plenty of parks and schools.
  24. T34Deto出戸Residential area with the Aeon Nagayoshi shopping center right at the station.
  25. T35Nagahara長原Quiet residential area built atop ancient burial mounds uncovered during subway construction.
  26. T36Yao-minami八尾南Southern terminus of the Tanimachi Line, near a small-aircraft airport.
